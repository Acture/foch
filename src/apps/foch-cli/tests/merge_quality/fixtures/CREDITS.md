# Merge-quality fixtures — credits & provenance

The committed fixture contains identifiers for a fixed cohort of selected
community Europa Universalis IV Steam Workshop mods. It does not redistribute
mod or game payloads. Product acceptance resolves installed items through the
Steam Workshop ACF and reads those source trees in place.

`workshop-product-cases-v2.json` is the complete denominator: 14 logical cases
covering 26 unique Workshop items. Local availability may prevent a run, but it
must not silently shrink that denominator.
